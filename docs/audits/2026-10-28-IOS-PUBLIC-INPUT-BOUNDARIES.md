## Privacy and interpretation

Capture is armed **only on the public mode picker**. Disposal or advancing to
permission/name input closes touch capture; public successor states record enums
only. The process-local512-row/10-number buffer never evicts older evidence.
Event callbacks perform no JSON encoding, logging, I/O, thread creation or polling.
Explicit post-action app-host capture is numeric only: no names, roles, identities,
room/session/credential values, hierarchy, exception text or touch identity.

UIKit coordinates are window points; Compose coordinates are local. Receipt
presence/count is not a causal diagnosis or proof that two samples identify the
same event. The decoder rejects duplicate/unknown/private fields, nonfinite or
nonintegral categorical values, malformed/out-of-bounds rows, backwards clocks,
overflow and invalid samples. The public-outcome comparison demands all60
original/Compose/native matrix entries; equal synthetic rows certify neither
source/runtime custody nor native touch/timing neutrality.

Apple's official UIGestureRecognizer/state-machine and touch-delay/prevention
contracts were fetched on October28 and retained with exact URL/body hashes in
local custody. The recognizer delays/cancels/prevents no touch, never recognizes,
and uses public `UIKit.UIGestureRecognizerSubclass`. **Adding a recognizer can
still perturb arbitration/timing**; a genuine native three-arm control is required.
