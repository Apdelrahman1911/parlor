## Description

Parlor turns phones at the table into one shared game night.

Play Whodunit, a cinematic cozy-noir murder mystery built around secret
dossiers, clues, accusation, and a final reveal. Or play Mafia, the classic
social-deduction contest of hidden roles, night actions, discussion, and vote.

Choose pass-and-play or connect nearby devices on the same local network. The
host approves every player before a game starts. There are no accounts, ads, or
in-app purchases, and gameplay does not require a public-internet multiplayer
service.

Parlor is built for people in the same room: read your role privately, look up
from the screen, talk, bluff, and decide who to trust.

## Privacy and networking copy

- No account or authenticated real-world identity.
- Local-network multiplayer only; no internet matchmaking, NAT traversal, or
  hosted relay.
- Player names and gameplay state are used for the local session.
- Analytics and crash reporting are separate opt-ins and default off.
- No advertising/tracking SDK or sale of data.

The final disclosure is derived from the signed artifact and
`docs/PRIVACY_AND_COMPLIANCE.md`, not this marketing draft.

## Rating and review notes

The intended audience is 13+. The stores determine the displayed rating from
their current questionnaires. Disclose murder-mystery themes, social deception,
and any mature/suggestive content accurately; there is no graphic violence.

Reviewer notes must explain:

- both multiplayer devices need to be on a mutually reachable local network;
- the host shows a room code and explicitly approves each peer;
- there are no credentials or test account;
- host exit ends the session; host migration and spectators are unsupported;
  and
- how to reach the pass-and-play path when a second device is unavailable.
