## Naming

- **App name:** Parlor
- **Subtitle (App Store):** Party Games for the Table
- **Short description (Play Store, ≤80 chars):** Cinematic party games for groups sitting together. Start with Whodunit.

## Full description (Play Store ≤4000 chars / App Store ≤4000 chars)

> The phone, in this game, becomes a host, a storyteller, and a secret-keeper.
>
> Parlor is a party-games app for groups in the same room. The first game inside is **Whodunit** — a cozy-noir murder mystery for 4–6 players where one of you is the killer, even *you* don't know who, and by sunrise you have to decide.
>
> Pass the phone around the table. Each player reads their secret dossier alone. Some of you have a secret. One of you killed Maxwell Hargrove. Every clue is a question, not an answer. Lie, accuse, and watch each other's faces.
>
> **Two ways to play.**
> · **Classic Vote** — investigate the full case, then vote once at the end. 25–35 min, 4–6 players.
> · **Elimination** — vote after every round. Find the killer before they survive. 15–25 min, 5–6 players.
>
> Six possible killers. Variant clues. Variant red herrings. Variant reveals. Play *The Last Dinner* five times and watch five different murders in the same house.
>
> No accounts. No ads. No always-online. One phone, one room, one evening.
